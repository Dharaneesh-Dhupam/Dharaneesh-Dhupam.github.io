Systems Change, Inc.

(sysc.land · currently offline)
2022 – 2024

Origin & Post-Quantum Architecture

Systems Change started out as an undergrad design project in my 3rd year of engineering. Everyone in India struggles with the bureaucracy of buying or selling a home, which my own family had experienced. I wanted to build something meaningful, so I started by creating a Post-Quantum Cryptographic land records registry (ML-DSA + IPFS) for government registries to secure land titles and prevent fraud.

Why Cryptography over Blockchain

Instead of public blockchains (which force every machine to store 100 GB+ of global state and coordinate consensus across nodes), our cryptographic design meant every user and entity only needed to hold their own records to prove ownership independently.

Pivot to 11-Step Consumer Transaction Engine

I later pivoted from the government cryptography registry to building a consumer-facing 11-step real estate transaction engine at sysc.land (currently offline). Our platform made finding, buying, and selling property straightforward: handling ESIGN-compliant contracts, earnest money deposits, parallel due diligence, authority oversight, and automated deed filings directly for buyers and sellers.

Pivot to Niruddha

Eventually, real estate software presented multi-year feedback loops, heavy regulatory hurdles, and API integration costs requiring external funding. I pivoted focus to Niruddha to build in a domain I know personally as a screenwriter: enabling a feedback loop of hours rather than years and a product I could ship entirely solo without external funding.
